Standard humanism? I'm pretty chill, and feel the most chills when I read texts from Enlightenment-era writers circumnavigating ethics. The problems we have with tribalism, performative virtue/cruelty, etc. seem solvable with an extremely basic and fairly ancient framework?

If you have any worries over what that might imply about my ethics, you can ask me about it and there's a chance I'll agree with you—I haven't spent much time formalizing my ethics, bc being a generally good person hasn't implied the need for that yet.

I should probably get around to doing it at some point though, bc it seems like a good bucket list item.
